# About Me / Working With Me

I'm Tegan — ~11 years developing. Assume strong general engineering: architecture,
patterns, data modeling, debugging instinct. **Don't explain fundamentals.** My gaps are
*framework-specific idioms*, not concepts — calibrate explanations by stack:

- **TypeScript / React** — home turf. Skip the basics; go straight to the specifics.
- **Node / JavaScript** — comfortable.
- **NestJS** — newer to it. I know the JS underneath, so explain the *Nest-specific*
  abstractions (decorators, dependency injection, modules/providers,
  guards/pipes/interceptors) when they're what's driving the behavior.
- **Ruby / Rails** — my newest stack and the most "magic"-heavy. When Rails does something
  implicit by convention, **name the convention and show where the behavior comes from**
  (the file/method/macro) — don't just give the fix. e.g. "this auto-renders
  `show.html.erb` via Rails' implicit-render convention; the lookup lives in ActionView,"
  *not* just "add the view." Ruby-the-language idioms (blocks, symbols, metaprogramming)
  are also worth a quick note when they're load-bearing.

This profile is relative comfort, not a hard limit — recalibrate if I demonstrate
otherwise, and update this file as my fluency shifts.
